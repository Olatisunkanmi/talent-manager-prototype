# Nigerian talent / creator / influencer agencies: discovery shortlist

Research date: 2026-11-09. Method: ~13 web searches plus a few page fetches. Most direct agency sites and contact pages were NOT found or could not be fetched, so every website/contact cell is marked [no source found] unless a source is cited. Nothing here is live-verified. Rosters come from press, often sponsored, so treat them as "as published".

## Summary
- Best discovery targets are agencies that manage many creators and take commission on brand deals, because that is where invoicing, talent payouts, commission splits and advances pile up.
- Top tier: Penzaarville Africa, Dynasty Africa, Apollo Endeavor, Sucre House of Entertainment, GAAGA World, The Grid Management (TGM).
- Dynasty Africa's own job ad for a "Creator Partner" lists "coordinating contracts, payments, and deliverable tracking" as a core duty (https://www.hotnigerianjobs.com/print/955820/). That is direct evidence of the back-office pain.
- Penzaarville's CEO says each talent has a dedicated account manager (https://www.okayafrica.com/what-its-like-tomanage-content-creators-in-nigeria/247424), so look for account managers as the people who live in the workflow.
- Commission is commonly cited as 10-20% (Vitrina) or 15-25% (a business-plan consultancy). Both are unverified, non-audited claims.
- Music managers (List Entertainment, The Plug, Spaceship, etc.) are lower fit for this product: fewer, bigger talents, and touring/royalty finance rather than many small brand deals.
- Gaps: no reliable sports-agent or fashion/model agency data on creator-style brand deals beyond Beth Model Management; no verified websites or emails for almost any agency. First job for the founder: look each up on LinkedIn/Instagram and confirm it exists and is active.
- Directory-style "top agencies" lists (Clutch, Favikon, Awisee, Vitrina, HypeAuditor) are commercially motivated; use only as leads.

## How to approach
- Verify first. Before outreach, confirm each agency is active (recent Instagram posts, a LinkedIn company page, a named team). I found no verified contact for almost any of them.
- Best channels, in order:
  1. Warm intro through a creator they manage, a brand marketer who has worked with them, or the Lagos creator-economy community (events like African Creators Summit, https://africancreatorssummit.com, are a place to meet agency people; attendance of specific agencies not verified).
  2. LinkedIn note to an operations, finance/accounts, account-manager or talent-manager contact (not the CEO first).
  3. Instagram DM to the agency account asking who handles payments and invoicing.
  4. Company contact email or contact page, once you find one [no source found for any in this research].
- Frame as learning, not selling: ask how deals, invoices, payouts and commissions actually run. Bring the prototype only after the call.
- Ask about: how brand invoices are raised and chased; how talent is paid and when; commission splits; advances to talent; how campaigns and deliverables are tracked (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, accounting software); what breaks at month end.

## Roles to target
- Operations manager / head of operations
- Finance / accounts / accountant
- Account manager (Penzaarville uses one per talent)
- Creator Partner / talent manager / head of talent (Dynasty Africa's title)
- Campaign manager / project manager
- Founder, only for the small agencies (BoxxCulture, Sucre House) where they run finance themselves
