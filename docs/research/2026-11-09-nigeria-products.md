# Prior art: products relevant to Talent Manager (Nigeria)
Method: 10 WebSearch queries (standard mode). Many sources are vendor/affiliate/SEO pages; prices conflict. URLs not live-verified.

## Summary
- No product found that is purpose-built for a Nigerian talent/influencer management agency's back office (commission settlement, advances, recoverable expenses, Naira part-payments). [no source found for any such product]
- Nigerian/African creator products found are brand-to-creator marketplaces (Cofluenxa, Creator Gigs Africa, Plaqad), not agency back-office tools. Whether they handle payouts/invoices is unknown.
- Global talent-manager tools (CreatorsJet, InfluenceFlow, Post for Rent, Roster, Influencer Hero) cover rosters, contracts and commission/payout tracking, priced in USD, no Naira/Paystack/WhatsApp focus evident.
- Nigerian invoicing tools (Invoice.ng, Kippa, Nomba Invoice, Bumpa, CashTraka, Zoho Invoice) handle Naira invoices and some WhatsApp sharing, but generic: no per-talent splits, advances, or expense recovery. Part-payment support unverified.
- Gateways (Paystack, Flutterwave) do not offer native partial-payment invoices per third-party sources; it is a developer workaround.

## Direct competitors vs adjacent
- Direct (Nigeria, agency back office): none found.
- Closest functional: CreatorsJet, InfluenceFlow, Post for Rent, Roster (talent-manager tools, USD, generic).
- Adjacent local: Invoice.ng, Kippa, Nomba, Bumpa, Zoho (invoicing); Cofluenxa, Creator Gigs Africa (marketplaces); Payorth/InvoChatng (WhatsApp invoicing, unverified).
- Adjacent infra: Paystack, Flutterwave, Payoneer, Wise, Metricool.

## White space
- Per-talent commission settlement ledger in Naira (brand pays, agency retains commission, remainder owed to talent).
- Advances to talent netted against payouts; recoverable vs absorbed expenses.
- Part-payment tracking on brand invoices tied to campaign and talent (gateways treat it as a developer workaround).
- WhatsApp-first creation of invoices/expenses into a structured agency ledger (WhatsApp invoice tools are single-business invoicing).
- Logging brand inquiries from talent inboxes.
- Post performance linked to campaign deliverables for agency reporting.
