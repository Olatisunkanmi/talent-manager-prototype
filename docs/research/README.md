# Research

Market research for Talent Manager. Both files are web-search output from 2026-11-09. Many sources are vendor, sponsored or directory pages, prices conflict, and no URL was live-verified. Treat all of it as leads to confirm, not facts.

- `2026-11-09-nigeria-products.md`: existing products (Nigerian invoicing and creator tools, global talent-manager tools, payment rails) and the white space.
- `2026-11-09-nigeria-agencies-shortlist.md`: 22 ranked Nigerian agencies, how to approach them, roles to target, and three outreach drafts.

Headline: no product was found built for a Nigerian talent agency's back office. The white space matches the PRD's core (per-talent commission ledger in Naira, advances netted against payouts, recoverable vs absorbed expenses, part-payments tied to a campaign, WhatsApp-first entry).
